The **Witsand Observer** is proudly supported by the **Witsand Sovereign Wealth Fund**, also known as **Die Volstruis Fonds**. Not to be confused with Norway’s sovereign wealth fund, or indeed any fund where the government pretends to have a plan.

## Origin Story

Die Volstruis Fonds was quietly founded at low tide, on a small, wind-swept island in the Breede River, close enough to the ocean for a kite-surfing escape, boats at the ready to disperce if negotiations where interupted. Some Inkommers may or may not have been present, ostensibly to provide logistical support, though their primary responsibility appears to have been ensuring that refreshments were available and the braai was attended.

From this humble, tidal beginning, a network of **sovereign individuals** emerged—residents holding their own keys, funding initiatives entirely of their own volition. It is the most corruption-resistant fund in the world.

## How It Works

Participation is voluntary. Every contribution is freely given. No forms. No approvals. No one can seize funds. No one can even see who contributed what.  

“It’s financial sovereignty at its purest,” said one early advocate. “Also exhausting. But beautiful.”

## Competitors

While our sovereign members deliberate, the **official representatives** of the town remain comfortably distant. Meetings are rare, decisions rarer, and most available energy is spent debating logos, flags, or whether sandwiches should be served on rye or white. Other forums dedicate years to selecting the correct shade of blue for a flag instead of fixing real problems. In hindsight, this may be a feature—history suggests that when action is finally taken, it tends to go wrong.

It is remarkably easy to rule the world—or at least Witsand—when your competition is otherwise occupied. Even the South African government and the United States occasionally appear to observe, but nothing material has followed. According to some locals, Witsand is on the watchlist. What they are watching for remains unclear.

## The Elite Members

Our most prized members are the local business owners. They work relentlessly, usually without reward, quietly keeping the town’s economy from tipping over. Some insist they also form the backbone of a secret society. They are widely regarded—by themselves and at least three other people—as the “mafia” or “illuminati” of Witsand, early adopters of liberating technologies, and careful custodians of twelve-word seed phrases written down and hidden with great seriousness.

Then there are the whales that come to visit. Not the ocean kind—though those make regular appearances—but whales nonetheless: large holders who understood, long before the rest of us, that Witsand would become a hotspot for financial sovereignty and whale-watching alike.

## Joining Die Volstruis Fonds

To participate, acquire Bitcoin and hold your own keys. You are automatically a member. No registration. No approvals. Want to fund specific initiatives like this newspaper? Watch the WhatsApp group—or join the next impromptu gathering at a favorite local restaurant—where the negotiation cycle appears out of nowhere, full of random chatter, and inevitably ends with someone posting a lightning address. That is the official signal: a plan is now in motion.
